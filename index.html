kami — Interactive Origami & Paper-Folding Simulator

kami is a free, open-source origami simulator that responds to your device's physical posture. Fold a foldable phone or close a laptop lid and the on-screen paper folds along the hinge. You can also fold and flip manually, change paper size and colors, and undo your folds. It runs in any modern web browser — no install or account required — and as a native macOS app.
