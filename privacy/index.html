Privacy Policy

Who we are

kami is made by Maksim Tatarchenkov. Contact: admin@maxwase.eu.

What we collect

No accounts, no personal details, no ads, no tracking. Device sensors (fold angle, motion) are used only on your device.

If you opt in, we collect anonymous usage events, error reports, device type, and approximate region to improve the app. You can opt out any time in settings.

Who processes it

Analytics is handled by PostHog (EU Cloud) on our behalf, over HTTPS. Nobody else receives it.

Your rights

Analytics is based on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Data is kept only as long as needed. You can ask us to access or delete it, and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

We do not knowingly collect data from children.
